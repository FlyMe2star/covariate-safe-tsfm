# Router decision

Route to **empirical-paper-writer** for an empirical ML paper.
Status: frozen P1b positive route stopped; evidence chain closed

The project makes testable claims about model behavior and a deployable input-policy
rule. A review-paper route would not answer the central question. The original P0
cross-backbone claim is stopped. P1a passed under the replacement framing, but
manuscript result drafting remained blocked until the frozen P1b evaluation was verified.
That evaluation has now failed its first fixed-sequence test and both backbone safety
requirements, so the positive applicability-gated method paper is stopped.

   the new frozen contract; no model inference and no sealed access.

   constant fallback, fixed-sequence comparisons, uncertainty, and safety rules are
   immutable before router-state construction and sealed evaluation.
4. **P2 paper-eligible evaluation** — untouched later origins or held-out tasks, paired uncertainty, ablations, efficiency.
5. **P3 manuscript** — backfill only verified artifacts into the evidence matrix.

## Frozen P1b decision

- Primary equal-group macro gain versus the strongest calibration-selected constant:
  -0.0113895; one-sided 95% lower bound: -0.0238545; failed.
- The conditional gated-versus-ungated test was not inferentially entered after that
  failure. Its descriptive macro difference was -0.0964359.
- Per-backbone safety failed for Chronos-2 (-0.0008515) and TimesFM 3 (-0.0219276).
- The same sealed outcomes cannot authorize threshold changes, router revision, or a
  replacement confirmatory aggregation.

The read-only archive audit passed all 20 checks, so the evidence chain is closed.
The recommended research decision is to pivot rather than market the failed method. A negative-results study
would require independently predeclared additional tasks/backbones, mechanism evidence,
and untouched evaluation data; the present result alone is not sufficient for the
target venue.
